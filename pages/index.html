P(H)NG

Pong in a 3D rendered world on a 2D playing field. Spin, smashes, dashes and specials, modifiers and arenas, against the computer or with up to six players at one machine or online.

Play in the browser Download Source on GitHub

What's in it

  • Arcade physics with spin, smashes that wind up, dashes, perfect hits and a power bar for a special that catches the ball to aim it.
  • Rallies heat up: every hit climbs a musical scale, the field glows hotter, and the deciding rally is replayed in slow motion.
  • Thirteen modifiers the ball collects on its way, from multi-ball, shields and portals to a ghost ball and a gravity well.
  • Arenas with bumpers and blocks, some of them moving.
  • Solo against the computer: one match, a ladder, a tournament of eight with their own ways of playing, endless, bricks and squash.
  • Two players on one keyboard or with gamepads, three to six on a polygon with a side for everybody.
  • Online over the internet with a four letter room code, or on the LAN.
  • Phong shading, neon glow and five themes, from neon to Game Boy green.

Play in the browser

The browser version is the whole game, with keys, gamepads and touch. It joins games over the internet and desktop hosts on the LAN by their address. It needs WebGL 2, which current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari have.
